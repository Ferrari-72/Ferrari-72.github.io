Scoring assumptions and limitations

Metric limit: nine proposed algorithms now have N=0, B=1/9, V=1 after known verifier-to-SAT routes are included. N=0 does not mean no algorithmic value: a reduction to 2-SAT would still give a polynomial algorithm.

The new-direct group asks mostly for polynomial algorithms via 2-SAT. The downstream group tests two-assumption chains. Their scores use different conditional baselines and are ranked separately. No research edge enters the official 76-question ranking.

The temporary research baseline includes ten documented polynomial-verifier-to-SAT support routes. These known upper bounds are not new open questions; their graph adapters remain subject to review. See the verifier arguments. This prevents inflating gains merely because an NP task was left isolated.

New task variants stay separate unless a semantic mapping is reviewed. Many equal scores reflect graph coverage and shared hubs, not equal scientific value.
