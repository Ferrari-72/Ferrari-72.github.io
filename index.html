About

Making intelligent systems answerable to evidence.

I am an undergraduate AI researcher at HKUST(GZ), visiting UC Berkeley in Fall 2026. My work connects LLM evaluation, executable verifiers, trustworthy research-agent workflows, and graph-search methods for combinatorial optimization.

I am especially interested in systems whose outputs can be checked independently instead of accepted on plausibility alone. My current work uses certificate-based verification, independent review, and explicit assurance gates. Formal specifications and machine-checkable proofs are a research direction I am actively preparing to explore more deeply.

Problem Reductions Benchmark

An evaluation harness for finding subtle logical bugs in combinatorial reduction rules. I developed the initial SWE-style and mini-SWE-agent runners, implemented the pred-based verifier and certificate pipeline, and added an AgentRunner interface, concurrent scheduling, trajectory archiving, and 99 automated tests. The benchmark reports reproducible bugs/Ktok and bugs/dollar metrics.

Research workflow design2026

AutoResearch for Reduction-Rule Discovery

A verify-first agentic pipeline built around fixed polynomial-time F/G contracts and at least 100 independently labeled instances before generation. The workflow preserves counterexamples across propose-test-diagnose rounds and separates no-shared-code verification, cold-context review, and optional Lean formalization into explicit assurance gates.
