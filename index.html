About

Making intelligent systems answerable to evidence.

I am a rising junior in Artificial Intelligence at HKUST(GZ), visiting UC Berkeley in Fall 2026. My work sits at the intersection of LLM reasoning, agentic systems, rigorous evaluation, and combinatorial problem-solving.

I am especially interested in systems whose outputs can be checked independently instead of accepted on plausibility alone. That has led me from benchmark and certificate-verifier design toward a growing interest in formal specifications and machine-checkable verification. I am currently building the foundations to explore formal methods more deeply.
